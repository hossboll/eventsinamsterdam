Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Information we collect

Stroooop is a static event guide. It does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information, and it does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Searching and filtering events happen in your browser and are not saved by this site.

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process technical request data, such as your IP address and the time and page requested, to deliver and protect the site. The hosting provider’s own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Cookies and local storage

Stroooop does not set or read cookies or use browser storage. Because the site does not use cookies, no cookie preference banner is currently shown.

Third-party services and links

The site loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these resources, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy for details.

Event listings may link to external venues and organizers. If you follow those links, their privacy policies and practices apply.

Contact
